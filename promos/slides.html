Retention

Policy, not
one number

Craft has one global maxRevisions. Visorr resolves policy across three axes, most specific first.

  • Stored in project config, so it deploys with the site
  • A pin outlives every policy — Visorr’s and Craft’s both
  • Preview and deletion are one resolver, never two
Most specific wins
1Entry · Homepage — keep 200Match
2Entry · News — keep 5, expire at 90dSkipped
3Any — keep 20, expire at 365dSkipped

The weight

Where the
weight is

Revision bloat is never spread evenly. It is two sections nobody thinks about, one of them full of Matrix blocks.

  • Broken down by section, element type and element
  • Sizes are estimates, and are labelled as such
  • What is reliable is the ratio between the rows
