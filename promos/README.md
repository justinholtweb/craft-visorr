# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Visorr listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as
the plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-visorr](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-visorr).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `visorr-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Slides

| # | Slide | Shows |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Cover — name, tagline, app icon, Free · Pro $49 badge | — |
| 2 | `Revision 47 · 14:12 · Ada` → `Hero · 6 words · +2 blocks` | the whole pitch, in one line |

| 4 | Five Matrix blocks labelled changed, moved, added | alignment by content, not by ID |

| 6 | Three policies, most specific first, plus a pin | retention as policy rather than a number |
| 7 | `visorr/report` and `visorr/prune/plan` in a terminal | where the weight actually is |

## Notes

Visorr is **freemium**, so the cover badge carries a price and slide 6 leans on retention rather
than on an "everything included" shelf. The only figure in the deck is the one on the cover — a
price change means editing slide 1 and nothing else. Keep it that way.

The **watermark is an outline, and the icon's tile is stripped** — two separate corrections, both
needed, and both inherited from the Blaster deck this one started as. The tile goes because at
slide scale its edge is a hard rectangle laid over the artwork. The fill goes because the visor is
one large solid mass: filled at 5% opacity it is a soft grey blob, and the seam between crown and
brim — the line that makes it read as a visor rather than as a shoe — is exactly the part that
disappears. Stroked, both pieces are line art and the seam is the strongest thing in the mark.

Two things had to change from the ray gun the deck was built around:

- **The mark is cropped in the SVG, not in CSS.** The visor is wide and short — 72×34 in the
  icon's 100-unit box, about 2.1:1 — so left in a square viewBox it sits as a band across the
  middle with empty space above and below, and any width that made it legible pushed the ends off
  the slide. The watermark's own `viewBox` is `12 31 76 38`, cropped to the glyph, so the box is
  the mark.
- **Stroke width is 1.6, and that is not the number it looks like.** The glyph paths sit inside a
  `scale(0.440063)` group — the transform that maps the original artwork's 227-unit tile onto a
  100-unit box — so stroke scales with them: 1.6 renders as 0.70 in the 100-unit space, about 11px
  once the mark reaches 1180px. Setting it to 0.7 directly gives a 5px hairline that vanishes at
  5% opacity. Change the transform and this number has to move with it.

The layout rules the Blaster deck was retuned for still apply here, and are still worth knowing:

- **One line per bullet, about 58 characters at 25px.** The copy column is 780px inside a 130px
  gutter and the panel starts at `left: 900px`, so a bullet that wraps runs at the panel and pushes
  the last point towards the footer lockup — and nothing clips, the text simply lands on top.
- **`.points li` is a flex container.** An inline `<span class="mono">` or `<em>` left loose in the
  text becomes a *sibling flex item* and picks up the 16px gap on both sides. Every point keeps its
  text in one `<span class="t">`.
- **A dashed 1px border does not read at 1920.** Slide 4 marks the moved block with one and slide 6
  the pinned revision; in both cases the `MOVED` / `PINNED` label is what actually carries the
  distinction, and the copy points at the label rather than at the dashes.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh`, which base64-inlines the two woff2 files in `assets/` —
Chrome's `file://` origin rules block them otherwise. It is gitignored; don't edit it.

`assets/icon.svg` is a copy of the plugin's `src/icon.svg`. Four copies of this artwork exist and
all four have to move together: `src/icon.svg`, `src/icon-mask.svg`, `promos/assets/icon.svg` and
`promos/assets/watermark.svg` — plus `web/images/plugins/visorr.svg` over in the justinholt.com
repo, which needs `ddev craft index-assets/all` after it is replaced. The mask and the watermark
are the same two paths under the same transform, one flat and one stroked.
